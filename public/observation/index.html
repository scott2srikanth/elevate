About these observations

Checks lighting and framing in photos and up to 12 video frames. Voice checks speech activity, volume and gaps; it does not transcribe speech. Unsupported video audio can be checked as a separate recording.

Results are experimental, with heuristic reliability. They do not measure personality, emotion, attractiveness or skill mastery.
