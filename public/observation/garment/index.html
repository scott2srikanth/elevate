How this works

A quantized FashionCLIP vision encoder and a small fixed garment classifier run here. Colour is estimated from visible pixels. This is an experimental integration; it is not trained on your wardrobe. It cannot establish size, fit, fabric, condition, authenticity or personal attractiveness. Full-body photos and complex backgrounds may give poor results. Uncertain categories require your choice.

Saved details follow your existing account sync settings; the image stays in memory and is discarded after adding or leaving. No sign-in is needed for analysis.
